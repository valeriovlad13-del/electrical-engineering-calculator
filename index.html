Engineering Note

These calculators are intended for educational and estimation purposes. The voltage-drop calculator uses the basic relationship VD = I × R and does not account for conductor material, conductor size, temperature, reactance, installation method, or electrical-code requirements. Always verify engineering calculations against applicable standards and project requirements.
